# CommunityHub Privacy Policy (Draft)

**Status: legal review required.** CommunityHub collects account identifiers, profile fields, community content, business/job/service/event records, uploaded media, CV documents, payment transaction references, device push tokens, diagnostics, and moderation records when users choose to provide them.

We use this data to authenticate accounts, provide community and marketplace features, process payments, prevent abuse, support users, and meet legal obligations. Supabase, hosting, email/SMS, payment, error-reporting, and push-notification vendors process data only as configured for these purposes. We do not store card numbers.

Users may access, correct, export where applicable, or delete their account through the app settings. Some financial, fraud, moderation, or legal records may be retained for the period required by law and then deleted or anonymized. CVs and private uploads are access-controlled and signed URLs are short-lived.
