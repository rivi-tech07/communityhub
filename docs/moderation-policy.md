# CommunityHub Moderation Policy (Draft)

**Status: operational and legal review required.** Prohibited content includes threats, harassment, hate, sexual exploitation, scams, impersonation, illegal goods/services, deceptive job or sponsorship claims, doxxing, malware, and abusive payment or promotion behavior.

Reports are triaged by trained moderators, with urgent safety matters escalated immediately. Actions may include warning, removal, demotion, restriction, suspension, account deletion, and referral to authorities. Moderation actions record actor, target, reason, timestamp, and report reference in the audit log.

Appeals, response targets, law-enforcement requests, child-safety handling, and jurisdiction-specific obligations must be finalized before launch.
