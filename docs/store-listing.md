# Store listing and reviewer materials (Draft)

**App name:** CommunityHub

**Short description:** Communities, local businesses, jobs, services, and events in one trusted place.

**Long description:** CommunityHub helps people connect with language, culture, location, interests, and professions; discover business catalogs and local services; explore jobs with clearly labeled sponsorship information; and register for community events. Users can report content and control profile privacy. Business, job, service, event, and promotion records are user-submitted unless explicitly marked as independently checked.

**Screenshots required:** login/onboarding, community feed, private community access, business directory, job sponsorship labels, service enquiry, event registration, account deletion, and payment failure/refund states. Use only approved sample data and no real personal information.

**Store declarations required:** privacy policy URL, support URL, account deletion URL, data safety answers, age rating, advertising/promotions disclosures, payment disclosures, content moderation contact, and reviewer test credentials.
