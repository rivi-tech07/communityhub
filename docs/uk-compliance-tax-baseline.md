# CommunityHub UK compliance and tax baseline

**Status: implementation baseline only — solicitor, data-protection specialist, accountant, and payment-provider review are required before launch.** This document does not provide legal or tax advice.

## Product classification to confirm

CommunityHub may operate as:

- a user-to-user social service with posts, photos, comments, and short videos;
- an online marketplace connecting customers with businesses, restaurants, hotels, service providers, employers, and delivery drivers;
- a payment and promotion intermediary; and
- a platform that may need UK seller due diligence and reporting.

The operator must confirm which supplies are made by CommunityHub and which are made by each trader. That decision affects contracts, VAT, receipts, refunds, liability, and platform reporting.

## Required UK policy and checkout work

- Show the legal entity name, registered address, support contact, company number where applicable, prices, delivery charges, cancellation terms, and applicable taxes before a customer commits.
- Make consumer and trader terms fair, prominent, readable, and saveable. Do not use blanket exclusions of statutory rights.
- Give order confirmations on a durable medium and retain the version of terms accepted for each transaction.
- Separate goods, restaurant food, hotel accommodation, services, event registrations, promotion purchases, and digital content because cancellation and consumer-rights treatment differs.
- For hot food and home delivery, collect the merchant’s tax treatment and display VAT-inclusive customer prices where the trader is VAT registered. Do not hard-code one tax rate for every product.
- Show whether the seller is a business or an individual/sole trader, and show the legal seller on receipts and pre-contract information.
- Label paid promotions and sponsored posts clearly. Do not describe a business, job, sponsor, or verification as checked unless evidence is recorded.
- Provide complaint, refund, cancellation, moderation appeal, and account/data-rights routes with response ownership and audit logs.

## Seller and tax data to collect

For businesses, collect and verify only what is necessary:

- legal name, trader type, trading name, registered/business address, country, and contact details;
- company registration number where applicable;
- VAT registration status and VAT number where applicable;
- tax country and a protected tax identifier workflow approved by the accountant and security review;
- seller terms/privacy versions accepted, acceptance timestamp, due-diligence status, verification source, and review date.

Do not store full National Insurance numbers, UTRs, bank credentials, or identity documents in ordinary client fields. Use a reviewed KYC/tax provider or encrypted server-side storage with restricted access and retention controls.

## Platform reporting decision

Because the app allows sellers to offer goods or services, determine whether CommunityHub is a reporting digital platform under the UK Platform Operators (Due Diligence and Reporting Requirements) rules. If in scope, build seller due diligence, annual reporting, seller copies, correction handling, five-year record retention, and HMRC submission ownership before launch.

## Required approvals before UK launch

- UK solicitor approval of Terms, marketplace contracts, trader terms, consumer flows, refunds, hotel/restaurant terms, moderation, and Online Safety Act scope.
- UK data-protection review of controller/processor roles, DPIA, children’s access, location tracking consent, CV handling, international transfers, retention, and ICO registration position.
- Accountant/tax adviser confirmation of VAT treatment by category, delivery charges, promotions, commissions, receipts, seller reporting, and UK/India cross-border flows.
- Payment-provider confirmation of merchant-of-record, chargebacks, refunds, customer funds, delivery disputes, and prohibited products.
- Food, alcohol, hotel, employment, and delivery-specific licensing/insurance checks for each business and location.
